When a Short stops you, Chumoku tells you why it worked, or rebuilds the same format as an outline for your channel. The answer opens in a panel beside the video, and is saved to your account.

  1. 01Open any Short on YouTubeAnalyze and Steal sit in the column above Like.
  2. 02AnalyzeA score out of 100, what hooks people and what loses them. 5 credits.
  3. 03StealThe moves that made it work, rewritten for your niche as a shot-by-shot outline. Saved to Ideas. 5 credits.
  4. 04ShortcutsAlt+Shift+A and Alt+Shift+S on the Short you're watching, or right-click any Short in a feed.
